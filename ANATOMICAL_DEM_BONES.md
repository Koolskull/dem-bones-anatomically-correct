# Dem Bones, Named Right

The dry-bones song with every adult human bone named correctly. **206 bones.** No foot bone, no leg bone, no head bone.

Each hip bone is one adult bone (ilium, ischium, and pubis already fused). The sacrum is one bone (five sacral vertebrae fused). The coccyx is one. The sternum is one (manubrium, body, and xiphoid). Those part names are sung inside the bone they belong to. They are not extra bones on top of the 206.

Extra sesamoids and wormian bones vary from person to person and are not in this count. The pisiform is in it, as a carpal. Both patellae are in it.
