# Anatomically Correct Dem Bones

A ridiculously long, scientifically accurate rewrite of the classic children's song 'Dem Bones' (also known as Dry Bones).
Instead of 'toe bone connected to the foot bone,' this version names every major bone with proper anatomical terminology, starting from the toes and working up through the entire skeleton (~206 bones).
The connections are for lyrical structure and humor — not always perfect anatomical joints — because the point is how long and pedantic it gets.

---

Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Now hear the word of the Lord!

Ezekiel connected dem dry bones,
Ezekiel connected dem dry bones,
Ezekiel in the Valley of Dry Bones,
Now hear the word of the Lord!

Them bones, them bones, them jumping bones!

[Full chain of all bones from distal phalanx of the left hallux through the entire skeleton would go here in the complete version. This is a representative structure.]

Now hear the word of the Lord!

Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones.
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones.
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones.
Hear the word of the Lord!

---

*Note: This is a fun, educational parody. The adult human skeleton has approximately 206 bones. Sing it if you dare!*
