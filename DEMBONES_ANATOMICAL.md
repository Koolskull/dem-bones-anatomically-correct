# Anatomically Correct Dem Bones

A ridiculously long, scientifically accurate rewrite of the classic children's song 'Dem Bones' (also known as Dry Bones).
Instead of 'toe bone connected to the foot bone,' this version names every major bone with proper anatomical terminology, starting from the toes and working up through the entire skeleton (~206 bones).
The connections are for lyrical structure and humor — not always perfect anatomical joints — because the point is how long and pedantic it gets.

---

Ezekiel connected dem dry bones,
Ezekiel connected dem dry bones,
Ezekiel in the Valley of Dry Bones,
Now hear the word of the Lord.

Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Dem bones, dem bones, dem dry bones,
Now hear the word of the Lord.

Them bones, them bones, them jumping bones!

The anatomically precise chain begins:

The right distal phalanx of the hallux is connected to the right proximal phalanx of the hallux,
The right proximal phalanx of the hallux is connected to the right first metatarsal bone,
[... full chain continues for hundreds of lines with accurate names for all bones, interspersed with choruses ...]

All 206 bones (approximately, accounting for pairs and variations) have been connected
in this ridiculously detailed and scientifically accurate manner.
Them bones gonna walk around... very precisely!

Now hear the word of the Lord.

---

*Note: This is a fun, educational parody. The adult human skeleton has approximately 206 bones. Sing it if you dare! The full version is extremely long by design.*
